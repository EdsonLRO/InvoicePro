## Activity-specific role record

| Activity | Tallyo role | Provider role position |
|---|---|---|
| Tallyo account, subscription, service security, support and legal compliance | Controller | Providers may be processors for hosted operations and independent controllers for their own account, security, legal or regulated purposes. |
| Business user's customer, recipient and invoice data | Processor for the business user | Supabase, Resend, Cloudflare and any applicable support system are subprocessors to the extent they process that data on Tallyo's instructions. |
| Tallyo subscription Billing | Controller for its subscriber relationship | Stripe has activity-specific processor and independent-controller roles under its DPA and regulated-services terms. |
| Connected-business customer payment | The connected business is merchant and controller for its customer/payment relationship; Tallyo processes limited integration and reconciliation data | Stripe and the connected account have their own role allocation for payment, fraud, identity, regulatory and financial processing. The public notice and business-user terms must not reduce Stripe to one blanket role. |
| Public AI Helper, if later enabled | Controller for the public visitor interaction | OpenAI is a processor under the applicable business terms/DPA; Tallyo account and customer data are outside the approved Helper purpose. |

## International-transfer rule

Do not publish a fixed list of processing countries as though it cannot change. The proposed notice should explain that providers and their subprocessors may process data in the UK and other countries, and that restricted transfers rely on an applicable adequacy regulation or contractual safeguard such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum to the EU SCCs.

The Owner must retain account-specific evidence of:

1. the accepted provider agreement and DPA version;
2. the contracting entity and selected services;
3. the current subprocessor list and notice route;
4. the transfer mechanism relied on for the relevant activity;
5. the plan, region and retention settings that affect the privacy notice; and
6. a review date and named monitor.
