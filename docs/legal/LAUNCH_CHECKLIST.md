# Proportionate UK small-business launch checklist

Status: Internal preparation. It is not legal approval and does not authorise publication.

Before unrestricted public onboarding:

- confirm the sole-trader business/controller identity and public contact/address decision;
- complete the ICO fee-checker decision;
- finalise an accurate Privacy Notice and business Terms;
- finalise business-user data-processing terms where required by the real controller/processor roles;
- maintain a current provider/subprocessor list and data-flow review for Supabase, Stripe, Resend, Cloudflare and hosting;
- approve a basic retention schedule and backup limitation explanation;
- approve fair cancellation, renewal, refund and failed-payment wording;
- prove account export, correction, deletion and rights-request procedures;
- prove complaint, incident and breach procedures;
- keep legal/privacy cases in a restricted record;
- verify product behaviour matches every published commitment;
- obtain Owner approval before legal publication.

Google Workspace Business Standard, Google Vault, a retained DPO, a cyber-response retainer and external legal approval for every ordinary change are not blanket technical launch requirements. External professional advice remains appropriate where a material unresolved legal question, consumer-subscription issue, significant complaint, breach, liability term, international expansion or regulator interaction requires it.

Never claim GDPR compliance, complete legal compliance, certification or full security. This checklist does not override a recorded legal block; the specific unresolved issue must be closed or explicitly escalated.
